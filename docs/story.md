# The Good Goblin — film script

A scroll-driven animated short. One continuous film, no cuts except where scripted; it ends on the actual product with no cut at all.

## Opening

Black screen.

*"Every campaign begins the same way…"*

We hear swords. Fire. Goblin screams. Dice rolling.

Then — one tiny goblin peeks around a rock. The party sees him. The DM smiles.

*"…and one goblin always gets away."*

He runs. Freeze. He turns toward the camera.

*"That was me."*

Immediately a mystery. Then the reveal — not "I help DMs." Instead:

*"You thought I ran away."*
*"I did."*
*"Straight to work."*

Now we follow him.

## Scene 1 — Lighting the world

The goblin runs ahead of the adventurers. He lights campfires. Sets lanterns. Opens cave doors. Places treasure. Scares ravens away. Puts mushrooms in a cave. Straightens signposts.

He is literally preparing tomorrow's adventure. The visual message: **someone is making the world ready.**

## Scene 2 — The world's unseen stagehand

The goblin reaches a forest. Pulls a giant rope. The entire forest rotates away like a theatre set. Behind it: a snowy mountain. He waves. Keeps running.

From here the camera follows *him* instead of the adventurers. He is literally changing scenes — the metaphor for what a DM does.

## Scene 3 — Helping the DM (the dice scene)

DM rolls. The die tumbles. **Natural 1.**

The instant it lands — *tick* — absolute silence. Everything freezes. The die is still wobbling slightly.

The goblin casually walks into frame. Looks at the die. Looks at us. Shrugs. Gives it the tiniest nudge. The die continues rolling. He dusts off his hands. Walks away.

The moment it lands on **20** — time resumes. The cheer from the table finishes naturally, as though nobody noticed the pause.

*"You're welcome."*

The audience knows time stopped; the characters don't.

## Scene 4 — More invisible jobs

Rapid montage. He is:

- drawing roads onto the map
- adding monsters to encounters
- hanging tavern signs
- planting trees
- filling treasure chests
- moving NPCs into villages
- putting candles on tables
- opening dungeon gates

The world's backstage crew, one goblin.

## Ending

The goblin reaches a cliff. Looks out over the whole world. The camera rises.

Everything begins flattening. Buildings become icons. Trees become illustrated. Mountains become ink. Roads become hand-drawn. Campfires become little painted flames.

The goblin smiles. Pulls a twenty-sided die from his pocket. Spins it. Throws it.

The die flies toward camera. It lands…

**Natural 20.**

The face of the d20 slowly morphs into the game board. Now you're on the actual Good Goblin map. Interactive begins.

**No cut.**
