# Good Goblin — Script v2 (scroll homepage)

Copied from the claude.ai artifact "Good Goblin Script v2" (54uZKvjpGeVJH426Fhqn4J) on 2026-11-06 so builders can read it.
Line wireframe of every frame: `docs/2026-11-06-homepage-wireframe.html`.

One floating island becomes the live map, the map turns out to be on the DM's laptop, and the laptop screen is where
you join. The goblin is the hand behind every interaction: you click, he does it, and the humans at the table never see him.
One continuous camera, no cuts.

## The goblin's five rules
1. He executes, he never decides. Every action is triggered by the visitor's click or scroll.
2. He is always on the island. He never floats in the UI and never leaves the laptop screen in the pull-back.
3. He is the only thing that moves when time stops (the dice gag).
4. He looks at the visitor exactly three times: hero hover, after the dice nudge, the final peek over the card edge.
5. He has no dialogue. Captions are his voice.

## Beat 0 · unit 0 · the hero, an island in the air
- Paper backdrop, no grid yet. Island floats right of centre, slowly drifting, low three-quarter angle so the underside shows.
  On it: signpost, campfire, one tree, one lantern post. Goblin on the near edge. A rope hangs off the island edge.
- Wordmark top left: GOOD GOBLIN / *The Dungeon Master's backstage.* / small: *Build the maps. Run the table. The goblin does the rest.*
- Hover: mouse rotates the island, yaw up to ±25°, pitch up to ±8°, eased so it feels heavy. Goblin's head follows the
  cursor. Touch: drag rotates.
- Idle: every 8–12 s one small act (leg swing, ember pop, bird on signpost). Never two at once.
- Copy: no caption. Small "scroll" hint with a down chevron.

## Beat 1 · units 0–2 · he docks the island
- On first scroll the goblin takes the rope and hauls. The island tilts to a three-quarter top-down view and descends
  until its near edge sits on the bottom of the viewport. Hover rotation hands off to scroll smoothly (never fights).
- A faint pencil grid fades/draws in across the whole backdrop; by unit 2 the island sits on the grid like a map on a table.
- Wordmark shrinks to top-left and stays for the pinned stage.
- Goblin ties the rope off on the signpost, dusts his hands, walks to centre.
- Scrubbed both ways. Caption at 1.2: **Every world needs a stagehand.**

## Beat 2 · units 2–8 · canvas showcase: you decide, he does
Rail of three chips at right edge: Light, Weather, Encounters. Real toggles on island state; the goblin's action is the
transition. Scroll walks through all three in order; clicking at any point replays that action and the new state sticks.
| Units | Icon | Goblin | Island result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2–4 | Light | climbs lantern post, lights it with a taper, slides down. 2nd click: blows it out from the ground | day paper → night paper; lantern pool of light on the grid; campfire brightens. Also the site's light/dark mode, persists |
| 4–6 | Weather | walks off the far edge, comes back dragging a cloud on a rope, parks it. 2nd click shoos it; 3rd: snow, he shakes the cloud | rain hatching on the island only, puddles; campfire gutters. Snow: ground white, cap on signpost |
| 6–8 | Encounters | drags in a sack, tips 3 monster tokens out, lines them up, nudges one with his foot | 3 enemy tokens with nameplates + faint threat rings. 2nd click swaps the set |
- Camera locked ¾ top-down; island fills lower two thirds. Chips: hand-drawn glyphs on paper; active = brass ring;
  hovering a chip makes the goblin glance at it.
- Reverse scroll: clicked state stays; only the default sequence rewinds. Caption at 2.4: **You decide. He does.**

## Beat 3 · units 8–13 · the table
- 8–10 Tokens arrive: 4 orange player tokens, one per half unit; goblin carries each in like a chess piece, sets it, straightens it. Nameplate under each. Turn pill top centre with first name.
- 10–11.5 First moves: active token slides 3 cells along the path; goblin runs ahead and peels back fog of war like a bedsheet. Monsters turn to face the party.
- 11.5–12.5 Dice gag: a red d20 rolls onto the island and lands on 1. Tick. Everything freezes and goes to pencil; the goblin, still in colour, walks to the die, looks at it, looks at us, shrugs, tips it to 20. Colour floods back. Turn pill: critical hit.
- 12.5–13 He steps back to the island edge and sits.
- Camera: slow push in through the beat; by the gag the die fills ~¼ of frame. Die roll fires once in real time at 11.5 and holds the wheel 1.2 s.
- Captions: 8.4 **Your players sit down. The table's already set.** · 12.4 (small) **You're welcome.**

## Beat 4 · units 13–16 · pull-back: it was on the DM's laptop
- 13–14.5 Camera pulls back and up in one move. The grid backdrop acquires edges, then a bezel: it was a laptop screen all along. Laptop on a round wooden table. Island keeps living on screen at the visitor's state.
- 14.5–16 Room fills in: DM behind the laptop (over-the-shoulder framing), 4 players, loose ink figures, dice, pencil, snacks, one warm lamp. Players look at the screen; DM at the players; nobody at the goblin.
- Silent site. Captions: 13.6 **Runs on the DM's machine. Nothing for players to install.** · 15.2 **They never see me. That's the job.**
